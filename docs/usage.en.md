# PhaiERP user guide

The left menu follows the work: Master data → Purchasing → Inventory → Production → Sales → Accounting, then System settings — every section starts with its overview (if any) — its top row has a card for each Transactions screen: what the screen does and the documents waiting for it (the first 3 numbers) — then two headings: **Setup** (data to define first) above (folded: click the heading to open it; the browser remembers, and it opens by itself on a setup page) and **Transactions** (documents, enquiries, reports) below — Accounting splits its transactions into Payables (AP), Receivables (AR), General ledger (GL) and Fixed assets (FA) (Sales sees **Finished goods stock**, Purchasing and Production see **Raw material stock**). Users only see the menus their role allows.

**Work queues:** a document page shows, above its list, what the step before left waiting for it (nothing waits, nothing shows); each row has a button that opens the next document filled in, some queues take several ticked rows of one party into one document, red rows are overdue — Sales: quotations → sales orders → shipments (one row per order line: ship-to place, enough stock or short, the open work or purchase order it waits for and the date it can ship) → invoices → billing notes; Purchasing: MRP purchases → requisitions → orders → receipts → landed cost; Accounting: receipts → payables → vouchers → payments, receivables to collect, documents to send by e-Tax, banks to reconcile, the period to close, assets to register and depreciation to post; Production: MRP make lines → work orders → release; Inventory: counts not posted

## Everyday use

- **Home** is the owner's overview (read only, no links): cash in bank, sales and gross profit this month, net profit year to date, orders to ship, receivables, payables, stock value, 12-month sales with gross margin % (against last year), receivables aging, an 8-week cash forecast, tax returns due and the open period, what needs attention (overdue receivables, customers over limit, stock below reorder point, late deliveries, work orders and vendors) and top customers with their gross profit — **Export PDF** gives the page on A4 landscape. Each person sees only what they may open.
- The left menu shrinks to icons with the menu button or the collapse button at its bottom.

- Lists: a search box (press `/` to jump to it), a status filter, click a column header to sort, click a row to open it. Each row ends with the same five icons: view (read-only), print, edit, undo (cancel / reverse), delete; one that does not apply to the row or that you may not use is greyed out.
- Save with **Save** or Enter; errors show under the field.
- A new document shows the number it will get beside the page title (e.g. PO2026100005); the number is only taken when you save, so leaving without saving keeps it for the next document.
- Fields picked from data (customer, vendor, item, document number, lot) list the choices on click; type to search, pick with the arrows + Enter or a click.
- Only irreversible actions ask for confirmation: delete, cancel, close period.
- Documents print on A4 with the company logo from **Print** (purchase order, quotation, delivery note, tax invoice, billing note, receipt, payment voucher, withholding tax certificate); **Save PDF** gives a PDF file named after the document number. The title and number sit top right, the customer or vendor beside a tinted box of the document's details, the item table runs down to the totals, and a row of signature boxes ends the page.
- **Signatures on documents** (every printed document): set your signature once from the user menu → **My signature** (draw it with a mouse or a finger, or upload a photo of it on white paper; it is trimmed, made transparent and kept as a PNG of at most 600 × 200 pixels, printing at most 4 cm wide), then use the **Signatures** button beside a document's print button. Under each signature box are **Sign** / **Ask to sign** / **Reject** / **Withdraw signature**, as your rights allow — our boxes (prepared by, sent by, received by) anyone allowed to print the document signs themselves; approval boxes (approved by, checked by, authorized signature) only the person asked to sign or an ADMIN; the customer's or vendor's boxes are left for paper. **Ask to sign** lists only people allowed to open that document; the request shows on **Waiting for me** (top bar and the **Documents to sign** page) and is e-mailed with a link when e-mail is set up (the e-Tax settings page) — the link always asks to log in first. Once signed, the signature, name and date show in the printed page and the PDF at once, with the **company stamp** (set on the Company page; printed only on signed documents sent to a customer or vendor). A document changed after signing prints without the signature until it is signed again. Who signed, when and from which machine is kept, with a copy of the signature image (changing your signature later leaves signed documents as they were) — this picture signature is not e-Tax's digital signature, which still signs with the certificate.
- Money shows 2 decimals; dates show as dd/mm/yyyy.
- **Attached files** (pictures or PDFs, several; pictures are made smaller before sending): purchase requisitions, purchase orders, goods receipts, landed costs, purchase returns, sales orders, shipments, sales returns, payables, payments, cash receipts, journal entries, bank reconciliations (the statement), filed VAT returns (PP.30), the PND report, stock counts, fixed assets, petty cash, and in the vendor, customer, item and QC dialogs — the **Attached files** box under a saved document says what belongs there (e.g. sales order: the customer's PO; payable: the vendor's tax invoice), and every table of these documents has a **paperclip at the end of each row** that opens the row's files in a dialog to view, attach or remove (green with a number = how many files). Rights are those of the screen (view = open, add/edit = attach, edit/delete = remove); deleting a document deletes its files — petty cash files are attached or removed only by the holder while the month is not counted.

## First setup

1. **System › Company** — name, address in the parts the Revenue Department forms use (no., moo, building, floor, room, village, soi, yaek, road, plus anything else; province, district and sub-district picked from the lists), logo, tax ID, branch (printed on documents; one address line is made from the parts), and the idle sign-out (minutes without mouse or keyboard; 0 = never).
2. **System › Document numbering** (or the table at the foot of each module's settings page, for that module's documents) — prefix, format and next number per document. The default is year-month + 4 digits, e.g. `PO2026100001`, starting at 0001 every month (year + 5 digits, or 6 digits that never restart, can be chosen).
3. **System › Accounting settings** — open period and default accounts (receivables, payables, sales, cost of sales, inventory, VAT, …).
4. **Master data** — items and materials, product lines, groups, types, units, currencies and exchange rates, taxes, payment terms; data used by one department sits at the top of its section, such as **Purchasing › Vendors**, **Inventory › Warehouses and locations**, **Production › Work centers, Machines**, **Sales › Customers**, **Accounting › Chart of accounts**. — customer, vendor and site / warehouse addresses (System › Sites, Address tab) are entered in parts like the company address (no., moo, building, floor, room, village, soi, yaek, road, extra address); documents print them as one line — fields the next documents need are required: a vendor / customer in Thailand needs its 13-digit tax ID, 5-digit branch, province, district, subdistrict and postcode (tax invoices, 50 ทวิ, ภ.ง.ด., VAT reports, e-Tax) and payment terms, currency and tax (due dates, document currency, VAT); a party abroad needs no Thai tax ID or address; a withholding tax code needs its account
5. **System › Roles and permissions**, then **Users** — view, add, edit, delete and print rights per screen (edit also covers a screen's weighty actions, e.g. posting a stock count, closing a period).

Opening stock: **Inventory › Stock count** — create a count for the warehouse with reason `OPEN`, add what is there line by line (location, item, lot, quantity, unit cost; an empty lot takes the count number), then **Post**: stock comes in at the cost given, booked against retained earnings.

## Inventory

- **Items and materials** — one screen, filtered by stock type (FG/RM from the product line) and product line.
- **Stock on hand** — by site, location and lot, or totals per part; filter by stock type and product line (the stock menus under Sales, Purchasing and Production open it already filtered). Click a line to view it: item, warehouse, location, lot, supplier lot, expiry, on hand, allocated, unit cost, value and the movements of that warehouse, location and lot (totals per part do not open; the stock pages opened from the Purchasing, Sales or Production menus show the line in a window instead of leaving for Inventory). Its buttons: **Convert to FG** (materials), **Transfer** (quantity, destination warehouse and location; value moved = quantity × the line's unit cost; lot and expiry go along) and **Edit** (reference S/N or pallet, qty per pack, status, grade, received date, expiry, lot number — an item without lot control is switched to lot control by giving a lot (shipments, issues and transfers then pick a lot), a line still without a lot moves its whole quantity into the lot given, an existing lot is renamed everywhere; lot-controlled items also have supplier lot and lot remark; expiry, supplier lot and remark apply to every place of that lot; warehouse and location change by Transfer; quantity and cost change only through movements).
- Lots always come from receiving: purchase receipt = tax invoice number, work order receipt = work order number, manual receipt = its document number (can be typed over) — issues pick a lot that is in stock (no adjustment: a decrease is an issue with a reason code, an increase a receipt).
- The stock pages carry the **Price adjust** button (finished goods) — no receive/issue buttons any more; stock comes in from purchase receipts, work orders or stock counts.
- **Price adjust** (finished goods only; a material keeps the cost it was received at): pick a part and its new cost; every place holding it gives the stock out at the old cost and takes it back at the new one (quantity unchanged).
- **Convert to FG** (button on a raw material line's view): raw materials are not sold as they are; convert them into a finished good first — quantity of material, the finished good, its quantity and location. The good's cost is the value of the material used.
- Every movement is kept in **Stock history**; each row shows the quantity before, the change (+ in / − out) and what is left after, for that warehouse, location and lot (0 +80 = 80, then 80 −30 = 50), and is posted to the ledger automatically.
- Items without **Kept in stock** (services, consumables, machines to register as fixed assets) have no on-hand and no stock history.
- **Stock counts** — create a count of the whole warehouse (year-end) or of chosen locations (a cycle count: only those are tagged and frozen); the system quantity is snapshotted into numbered tags, **Print tags** (eight per page, no system quantity: a blind count) and hang one on every pile; key the counts in from the returned tags with the **Counted by** name. A tag not used is **voided** (it stays on the list, so tag numbers always add up); one that is far off is **recounted** (the first count is kept). Posting needs every tag counted or voided — it names the tags still out.
- Average costing, recalculated on every receipt. Stock cannot go negative (changeable in **Inventory settings**).

## Purchasing

Lines of requisitions, purchase orders, quotations and sales orders take an item from the list or any text for something not in the system (freight, installation) with its own description and unit. Free text and items not kept in stock are received or shipped without a location and never touch stock; on the purchase side they are expensed (the product line purchase account, or **Expense for non-stock items** in the ledger settings).

```
Requisition → Purchase order → Receipt → Payable → Payment
```

- **Requisitions** have no approval step (the printed copy is signed): once saved they can become purchase orders, or they come from MRP. **Make PO** makes one order per **preferred vendor** of the lines; lines without one go to the vendor you are asked for (not asked when every line has one).
- **Purchase orders** are priced before VAT and may be in a foreign currency. There is no approval step: once saved they can be received; they can be edited until goods arrive. After a receipt, the order shows its **receipt history** on the right.
- **Scheduled purchase / sales orders** (Supplier / Customer Scheduling as in the big systems): set **Order type = Scheduled** (a long price agreement delivered in instalments), save, then **Delivery schedule** (or the menus **Purchasing › Supplier schedules** / **Sales › Customer schedules**, every scheduled order with its release in use and next delivery) → **New release**: each release (id = release date-number) is the whole schedule, the newest in use, the old ones kept and compared date by date; it has a release date, **Firm until** (instalments up to it are firm, later ones a forecast; 14 days by default), a source and a reference. On the purchase side **New release from MRP** drafts it (firm or received instalments stay, later ones become what MRP needs); on the sales side it is keyed in from the customer's release (large customers send it by EDI) or set by us. Releases print for the partner. The line's ordered quantity is the sum of the release in use, what arrived / shipped counts against the earliest first, and the order stays open after the last instalment until **Close**. MRP plans on each instalment's date and the printed order lists them.
- **Receipts** may be partial; over-receipt is allowed up to the set tolerance. Stock comes in at the net price × the order's exchange rate. Lot-controlled items take the tax invoice number as their lot (it can be typed over). Each line has a **Per pack** field (quantity per pack/bundle, 1 by default) kept on the stock line received. A saved receipt opens as a read-only view (Back, Waiting for payable / Payable created badge, Create payable, Print / PDF); it cannot be edited since stock and the ledger are already posted.
- **Landed costs** (freight, duty) are spread into the cost of the receipt's items. Click a receipt in the list to open its view (No costs yet / Waiting to allocate / Allocated badge, Allocate, Print / PDF, Edit); press Edit to enter the costs — allocated ones stay locked.

## Sales

```
Quotation → Sales order → Shipment → Invoice → Billing note → Cash receipt
```

- **Prices** come from the customer's price list, then list `ALL`, then the part price; they refresh when the quantity changes.
- Sales orders have no approval step; an order over the credit limit is saved with a warning.
- Shipment: opens with what is left to ship and on hand filled in (enough stock ships all in one), the location / lot chosen for it (oldest expiry first); when one lot is not enough the line splits into more rows, and the invoice puts them back into one line per order line; **Confirm shipment** asks once more.
- After **Confirm shipment** the invoice opens filled from the shipment, to check and change (date, due date, customer PO, remarks, price, discount) before saving; one invoice per shipment. An invoice cancelled right away (the last number, not sent by e-Tax, nothing referring to it) can be deleted and its number is used again.
- An invoice can be cancelled only while unpaid and not on an open billing note.

## Production

1. **Product structure** per part: pick the made item once and edit two tabs, **Materials** (quantity per unit, scrap % — start/end dates and remarks open from a line's ⋯) and **Steps** (work center, setup and run hours); one Save stores both. The header shows one unit's cost: materials (qty × (1 + scrap) × current cost) + steps (run hours × the work center's labor + machine + overhead rate; setup not included).
2. **Work order**: create → release → **material issue slip** (series MI) → receive finished goods (good, scrap, inspector — each receipt makes a QC record and books the routing hours by itself) → close. One page, no tabs, top to bottom: materials · issue/return slips · receive · labor · cost. **Edit** is available until it is released.
   Materials open with what is left to issue, oldest-expiry lot first, split over lots by themselves; saving makes one issue slip (printable for requester / store / approver). Once everything is issued the inputs hide — **Issue more** beyond the bill needs a reason; **Return materials** puts back into a lot the order took from, no more than it took, at its issue cost (return slip); a wrong slip is cancelled from its row — everything goes back where it came from and the slip stays, marked cancelled (until the order closes).
   Labor: nobody times anyone — each receipt books the routing's standard hours (run time × (good + scrap) + set-up once) at the work-centre rates; the labor section shows hours, labor and overhead per step (read only); a wrong standard is fixed on the routing. The month's real wages go in a journal (Dr direct labor); what is left on that account is the month's labor efficiency.
   Cost: output goes into stock at the standard unit cost (bill × issue cost + routing hours × work-centre rates); the table shows standard / actual / variance (material, labor, overhead); closing books what is left in WIP to **production variance** (50104, GL settings).
3. **MRP** plans from open sales orders and unissued materials, then creates requisitions or work orders from the lines you pick. As in the big ERP systems the planner presses **Run MRP** (items level by level down the bills of material, netted against stock (keeping the safety stock) and scheduled receipts, shortages become planned orders sized by the lot rules, order date = due − lead time); the result stands until the next run: **planned orders** (tick them into a requisition or work orders), **action messages** (release / reschedule in or expedite / reschedule out / cancel open orders) and the **MRP record** of every item, enough or not — click a row for the textbook weekly grid (gross requirements, scheduled receipts, projected on hand, net requirements, planned order receipts and releases) and the detail behind each number.
   **What to make**: the production dashboard lists the open sales-order lines of items made in-house that the stock does not cover, with the customer, wanted date, what comes from stock, the work orders making it and when they finish — **open a work order** (+ quantity) / **late** / **on time** (matched the same way as the shipment queue). The two queues on the work order page show **For**: the sales order and customer.
4. **Employees** (Production → Setup → Production employees): there is no payroll — the users who sign in to work each process are the staff. Set the **Sign-in user** of an employee who signs in (one employee per user); then "requested by" (requisitions) and "inspector" (QC) start with the signed-in person's name — pick another employee or type a name — and labor on a work order starts on their employee number.

## Accounting

The Accounting menu follows the work — **Payables (AP)**: **payable (AP)** (every line can carry a withholding code, the vendor's by default — the payment fills its withholding table from the lines; a payable still owed and on no voucher can be **Edit**ed, the ledger getting only the difference) from receipts (the queue of receipts waiting on its page: tick receipts of one vendor and make a payable) or by hand, with its **payable account** (the vendor's by default), or a vendor **credit note** naming an earlier payable → **voucher (VO)** gathering payables of one vendor, printed for signing; it posts Dr each payable's account / Cr vouchers payable → **payment (PV)** of one or more vouchers (a voucher's Pay button opens it filled in), posting Dr vouchers payable / Cr bank / Cr withholding tax → **input VAT report** → withholding tax certificates; **Receivables (AR)**: sales invoices (from shipments) and hand-made **invoices / credit notes / debit notes** (no shipment needed; a credit or debit note names an earlier invoice of the same customer — a credit note is numbered CN and reduces the receivable, output VAT and sales, a debit note is numbered DN and adds like an invoice — and both need a **reason** from the Revenue Department codes, e.g. CDNG02 goods damaged, DBNG01 goods price increased; an "other" reason must be written in the remarks; the reason is printed on the note and used in the e-Tax XML) → sales journal (the entries invoices post) → **output VAT report** → receivables → cash receipts (open credit notes are set against invoices on the same receipt; the net received cannot be negative); then Fixed assets (FA) and General ledger (GL). The VAT reports take a tax month and list invoice number, name, tax ID, branch, value and VAT in baht, printed as a landscape PDF for the VAT return (P.P.30).

**Petty cash** (Accounting → Cash and bank): everyone with this screen has their own petty cash box and works on their own box only (no limit — what they take in is theirs to account for). Entries: **withdraw from bank** (Dr petty cash / Cr bank), **receive from another holder** (the receiver records it; two entries with their own numbers, handed − / received +, no ledger entry), **pay an expense** (lines of account, cost centre and amount before VAT; a full tax invoice splits out input VAT for the purchase VAT report; withholding by the line's code needs a vendor and gives a certificate in the same series as payments) **receive money** from outside such as scrap sold (Dr petty cash / Cr other income) and **return to bank** — money passed on cannot be changed (the giver receives it back), every bill needs a receipt number, tax invoice or file before the count, and the page lists everyone's petty cash for the month (brought forward, entries, balances per holder and in all), exportable to Excel — payments, cash receipts and deposits can use the **CASH petty cash (of whoever makes it)** account, taking the cash from or putting it in the maker's box (it is not a bank to reconcile) — each has a PC number in the month of its date, pictures or PDFs can be attached, and it prints; only its maker can edit (keeping the number) or cancel it, while its month is not counted — forgot an entry after counting: undo the count, enter or correct it, count again. At every month end the holder **counts** the cash (notes and coins); a difference posts to cash over and short and the month locks — until counted, the box takes nothing dated in the next month and the period cannot be closed. **Petty cash register** prints the month with the count.

**Bank accounts** (Accounting → Cash and bank): the list shows today's balance (from the ledger), the last reconciliation and the lines waiting for one; a row opens its **bank book** — month by month: brought forward, deposits, withdrawals, running balance, each line's reconciliation date and the month-end balance; a line opens its journal entry; exports to Excel and prints (the petty cash row opens petty cash). The pencil edits the set-up.

**Bank reconciliation** (Accounting → Cash and bank): pick the bank and statement date, then tick the book lines that are on the statement (click anywhere on the row) — last reconciled balance + ticked lines = the **balance after**, which you check against the real statement yourself, then save (it is kept as that statement's balance); unticked lines wait for the next statement. The history below prints the **bank reconciliation statement** (statement balance + deposits in transit − outstanding payments = book balance) and undoes only the latest one. Amounts are the baht on the bank's GL account.

**e-Tax Invoice by Email** (for VAT registrants with revenue up to 30 million baht a year whose email is registered with the Revenue Department): set it up in Accounting → Setup → **e-Tax / email settings** — sender email (the registered one), SMTP server, port, encryption, user name and password (kept in config.php, not the database), year in the subject (Buddhist/Gregorian) and Cc `csemail@etax.teda.th`. Use **Send test** before switching it on. Invoice and credit note pages then show **Send e-Tax**: enter the buyer's email (defaults to the customer's) and the printed tax invoice goes as a **PDF/A-3B** as ETDA requires (at most 3 MB), one document per email, To one buyer, Cc the ETDA system that time-stamps it and forwards it. Subjects follow `[date][INV][number]`, credit notes `[date][CDN][number][invoice]`, debit notes `[date][DBN][number][invoice]`, cancelled invoices `[date][CIV][number]`. Every attempt, sent or failed, is listed in **e-Tax Invoice (email)** under Receivables (the menu shows once email is on, a certificate is in for XML, or something was sent).

**e-Tax Invoice as XML** (for businesses approved by the Revenue Department for e-Tax Invoice & e-Receipt — one route only, not together with email): upload the certificate (.p12/.pfx from a CA under the Thailand NRCA such as Thai Digital ID, INET, NT, ThaiCA) and its password in **e-Tax / email settings**; invoice and credit note pages then show **XML e-Tax**, which downloads the ขมธอ.3-2560 XML signed with XAdES-BES (RSA-SHA512) — tax invoice (388), credit note (81) and debit note (80) with the original value, corrected value, difference the reason code chosen on the note and the remarks as its text (no remarks: the words of the code); the cash receipt page has **XML e-Tax** too, a receipt (T01) with one line per invoice paid (a credit note set against it is an allowance). A signed file is kept and handed out again unchanged. The **e-Tax Invoice (email)** page has **XML of the month**: a zip of every document of that month (tax invoices, credit and debit notes, receipts) (the ones that cannot be made are listed in errors.txt) for "Upload XML" on etax.rd.go.th by the 15th of the next month (3 MB per file). Company data must be complete: 13-digit tax id, 5-digit branch, an address starting with the house number plus subdistrict, district, province and postcode (turned into register codes), and customers need an address and postcode; a cancelled document has no XML — issue a credit note instead.

**PND 3 / 53 report** (Accounting → Payables): pick the form (PND 53 companies / PND 3 individuals) and month to list the withholding certificates issued by payments that month (cancelled ones left out) with tax id, branch, name and address, date paid, income type, rate, amount paid and tax withheld; print it on the Revenue Department's own forms — the return (A4 portrait: tax id, branch, address, month ticked, section remitted under, payees and sheets attached, totals) and the attachment sheets (A4 landscape, six payees a sheet, three lines each, with rate and condition); the sequence number is per payee and matches the one on the 50 Tawi certificates — for filing by the 7th of the next month.

Documents post to the ledger by themselves:

| Receipt | Dr Inventory / Cr Goods received not invoiced |
| Payable | Dr Goods received not invoiced or expense, Dr Input VAT / Cr Payables |

**Customer deposits** (button on a sales order): the amount received is split into value and VAT and printed as a deposit receipt / tax invoice (Dr bank / Cr customer deposits, Cr output VAT); the order's tax invoices deduct it before VAT. **Services**: a hand-made invoice can make its VAT due on payment; each receipt then moves its share from VAT not yet due to output VAT and prints as receipt / tax invoice.

New screens: **Purchase returns** (from a goods receipt; deducted on the vendor invoice or cleared by the vendor credit note), **Vendor evaluation** (on time %, returned %, lead time, grade A/B/C), **Sales returns** (from a shipment; the credit note is made from it on the AR invoices page), **Stock card** (report of goods and raw materials, s.87(3); all items grouped per item, or one item / FG / RM, with the total value), **VAT return (PP.30)**, **Financial statements** (balance sheet and income statement) and **Learn ERP** (module map, document chains with official Thai names and entries, glossary).

- **Journal** lists every entry by type; manual entries must balance and cannot use control accounts; correct them with **Reverse**.
- **Receivables** shows ageing: not due / 1–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / over 90 days.
- **Payments** take the form (PND 3 / PND 53) and the withholding tax codes in the withholding table under the lines (the first rate's base is filled from the amount before VAT); one payment may withhold at several rates, e.g. services 3% and transport 1% — **Add rate** and split the base (together no more than the payment, each code once); all rates go on one certificate, the first under its income row and a further one of the same row on "6. Others (specify)", each with its rate, amount paid and tax, and as separate lines in the PND report; a certificate is issued — printed on the Revenue Department's own 50 Tawi form in its two copies (copy 1 the payee files with the tax return, copy 2 the payee keeps), with the tax ids in the 13 boxes, the PND form ticked, the sequence number matching that month's PND report, and the amount on the income row set on the **withholding tax code** ("Income row on the 50 Tawi form", row 5 by default: hire of work, advertising, rent, transport, services).
- **Trial balance** for any date range; click an account for its ledger, click a line to open the entry.
- **Close period** (on the trial balance page) once the month has ended — nothing can be posted into it afterwards. The fiscal year is the calendar year.

## Ask AI

**Ask AI** answers questions in plain language, e.g. “purchase orders not yet received”, “customers more than 30 days overdue”, “which parts are running low”.

- It reads data through the same API as the screens and sees only what the person asking may see.
- It only reads; it never creates or changes data.
- Each answer lists where the data came from — check important figures on the screen before relying on them.
- Administrators choose the provider (Claude / Gemini), API key and prices in **System › AI settings**, where the cost per user is also shown.

## Users and security

- One role per user. Users are deactivated, not deleted; every change is kept in the audit log.
- Five wrong passwords within 15 minutes lock the account for a while.
- Change your own password from your name at the top right.


List grids provide a **Columns** picker with shared preferences saved per table in the database; reopening or refreshing the page loads the shared layout. Use **Reset columns** to restore defaults. Stock history records inventory transactions; optional fields include transaction ID, time, document line, unit price, unit cost, signed movement value (quantity × cost), and remarks.

List-grid search includes all public result fields, including optional columns, before pagination. The column picker is an icon at the right of the table header row.

The **Export to Excel** icon (beside the column picker) downloads a .xlsx of every row matching the current search and filters (not just the visible page), with the shown columns in screen order; numbers and dates stay real Excel values, the header is frozen and filtered.

Master data screens add an **Import from Excel** icon for users who may add or edit: **Template** gives a sheet whose headers match the form (red = required, second sheet = allowed values), Master data screens export every form field just like the template (regardless of the shown columns), so an exported file can be edited and imported back as is. Rows whose key exists are updated (columns missing from the file are left alone, empty cells clear the value); other rows are added. If any row is wrong nothing is saved and the dialog lists "Row N · field: message". Up to 5,000 rows per import.

Date fields use a Thai calendar with Buddhist years, storing Gregorian YYYY-MM-DD values. Shared grids support hover and details via click or Enter. Master records and standard documents open for viewing; Edit is available according to permissions and status. Fresh installs include these features; existing column choices require migrating the database.

List footers offer 10, 20, 50, 100, 300 or 500 rows per page (default 20). Changing page size returns to page one while preserving search, filters and sorting.

View mode displays plain text without input boxes. Edit reveals the form. Documents include a Back button to their list.

The sidebar clock shows the day, short month, year (Buddhist era in Thai) and time (e.g. 10 Oct 2026 06:49:20) in Asia/Bangkok, or HH:mm when collapsed on desktop. Sidebar expansion requires a toggle click; hovering does not expand it.

**Pinned menus:** hover a menu item and click the pin on its right to place it right after "Learn ERP" (in pinned order); click the pin again to remove it. Pins belong to each user and follow them to any device.

Print routes open PDFs directly when server-side Edge/Chrome is available. Additional document types have Print/PDF buttons subject to print permission. Use ?html=1 for the HTML preview.

### Product Lines and sidebar folders

Open Master Data → Product Lines (/product-lines), set code/name, stock classification FG or RM, tax and active status, then set the company account mappings in the Accounts tab (inventory, sales, COGS, purchase and WIP). Codes may be business-specific, such as FG-FURNITURE or RM-STEEL. Assign the Product Line on each item/material; its classification is what the stock type filter on Items and materials and on stock uses, with no duplicate item type selector. Purchased/Manufactured source is independent. New items require an active classified line.

A new install brings lines with accounts: **FG** (inventory 12101, sales 40101, COGS 50101, WIP 12102), **RM** (12103) and fixed asset (**FA**) lines BD (buildings), MC (machinery), OF (office equipment) and VH (vehicles), each tied to an installed asset category with asset, accumulated depreciation, depreciation (50901) and disposal gain/loss (40204) accounts. Buy a machine as an item on an FA line through a purchase order; the receipt posts to the category asset account, then register the asset as already booked with the receipt as reference. The asset register has a **product line made** field and **Production › Machines** has a **fixed asset** field linking each machine to its asset. Existing GEN records count as FG until explicitly reassigned. If GEN contains both goods and materials, assign separate lines per item instead of classifying all of GEN. A line classification change affects all linked items, but does not rewrite historical GL entries. Configure separate FG/RM account mappings according to the company chart of accounts. Opening a sidebar folder closes the other folders; clicking it again closes it, on desktop and mobile.

### Fixed Assets

Configure Accounting → Asset Categories with SL/DB/NONE, useful life, annual DB rate and distinct active non-control asset/accumulated-depreciation/expense/gain-loss accounts. Add an asset register with cost, salvage, acquired/in-service dates, site/location, cost centre/custodian and source purchase reference — an asset bought on a purchase order with an FA product-line item picks the goods receipt (the vendor's invoice), filling vendor, date acquired, cost and category (the receipt already posted the cost, so register it as BOOKED). Save a draft, attach its documents (invoice, warranty, pictures) under **Attached files**, review, then activate. Use BOOKED for cost already in AP/GL (including opening depreciation with an opening month); POST creates cost debit against an explicit credit account without AP/VAT automation.

Monthly depreciation previews and atomic batches require contiguous completed months in an open period; duplicates are rejected. SL=(cost−salvage)/life months; DB=current NBV×annual rate/12. Full in-service month, no daily proration; final useful-life month settles to salvage. Transfer changes physical/organisational assignment only. Dispose after completing prior-month depreciation, with no depreciation in disposal month; proceeds and gain/loss are posted while removing cost/accumulation. Use a clearing account with separate sales/VAT/cash documents to avoid duplicate proceeds. Correct only the latest effective event by reversal in an open period. Print asset cards, current register and monthly depreciation reports with the company logo.

Edit permission authorizes drafts, transfers and the financial actions; print is separate. No separate tax book, revaluation or automatic purchase-invoice integration.

## User menu and themes

**Language**: the flag beside **Help** (top right) switches Thai / English — Thai screens show a US flag (EN), English screens a Thai flag (TH); a click reloads the page in that language and keeps it with the user. The login page has the same flag. Printed documents (tax invoices, 50 Tawi, PND, PP 30, reports) are always in Thai, as Thai tax law expects, and entered data (customer and item names) shows as entered — the help pages and Learn ERP are in English too.

The user menu (top right) opens on a card with your picture and name: **My profile** (name, e-mail, phones and a PNG/JPG/WebP picture of any size, made small in the browser — initials without one), **Change password**, and **Theme**: six colour dots — **Bamboo** (green, the default), **Mist** (white and grey), **Indigo** (blue), **Butterfly pea** (purple), **Terracotta** (brick orange) and **Night bamboo** (dark) — a click changes it at once (the menu stays open to try another) and it is kept with the user on every device. Printed documents and PDFs stay white.
